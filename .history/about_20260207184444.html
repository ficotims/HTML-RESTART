About Iman Musa

Iman Musa is a fictional character created for the purpose of this HTML crash course. She is portrayed as a web developer and designer with a passion for creating beautiful and functional websites. Iman has a strong background in computer science and has worked on various projects ranging from small business websites to large-scale web applications.

In her free time, Iman enjoys exploring new technologies, learning about user experience design, and contributing to open-source projects. She is also an advocate for diversity and inclusion in the tech industry.
